Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I customize my workout plan?

Yes! You can edit, add, or remove exercises within your current plan to suit your preferences.

2. Are meal plans included in AuraFit?

Yes, we offer meal plans tailored to your goals — like weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance.

3. What is the AuraFit Chat feature?

It's an AI-powered assistant that helps answer your fitness, nutrition, and progress questions instantly.

4. What is BMI and why does it matter?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple way to assess if you're in a healthy weight range based on your height and weight.

5.Can the chat coach create custom plans for me?

Yes, our AI coach can generate custom workouts or meals based on your preferences and fitness level.

6.How do I calculate my BMI?

Go to the BMI section in the app, enter your height and weight, and we’ll calculate it for you.

7. Can I change the auto-suggested goals?

Yes, you can manually update your fitness or nutrition goals at any time from the profile or goal setup page. AuraFit’s suggestions are a starting point — your goals are always in your control.
